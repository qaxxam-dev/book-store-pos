# Screen: Sales History

## Purpose
The **Sales History Screen** lists all customer sales transactions.

## Widgets

- **Filter Actions**: Filter by Customer and Date Range.
- **Sales List**: Cards displaying:
  - Bill Number
  - Date & Time
  - Total Amount (in green)
  - Payment Method

## Interactions

- **Tap Sale**: Opens `SaleDetailsScreen`.
- **Filter**: Tap filter icon to narrow down results.
