# Screen: Customer Entry (Add/Edit)

## Purpose
The **Customer Entry Screen** is a simple form to create or update customer profiles.

## Widgets

- **Form Fields**:
  - **Name**: Customer name.
  - **Phone**: Contact number.
- **Save/Update Button**: Commits changes.

## Interactions

- **Save**: Creates or updates record in database.
